Your Windows domain password expires every 90 days. You get a reminder 14 days before.

## Reset it yourself

1. Open https://sso.example.com/password from any company device.
2. Sign in with your current password and confirm with the MFA prompt on your phone.
3. Choose a new password: at least 14 characters, no reuse of the last 10 passwords.
4. Lock and unlock your laptop once so the new password is cached locally.

## If you are already locked out

Call the helpdesk on extension 4500. The agent verifies your identity with your
employee number and the last four digits of your mobile number, then issues a
one-time password that must be changed at the next sign-in.

## Common problems

- **VPN keeps asking for the old password** - reconnect the VPN after the reset, see kb-002.
- **Mac users** - the FileVault password is separate and does not change automatically.
