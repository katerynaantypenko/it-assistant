Every sign-in to a company system requires a second factor from the Authenticator app.
When you replace your phone you must re-enroll it.

## If you still have the old phone

1. Go to https://sso.example.com/security on your laptop.
2. Choose **Add sign-in method -> Authenticator app**.
3. Approve the confirmation prompt on the old phone.
4. Scan the QR code with the app on the new phone.
5. Remove the old device from the list once the new one works.

## If the old phone is lost or wiped

You cannot self-serve, because the only remaining factor is gone. Call the helpdesk on
extension 4500 from a number we already know, or visit the IT desk in person with your
badge. The agent resets the MFA registration and you enroll again from scratch.

Report a lost phone as a security incident as well - see kb-008 for the reporting channel.
