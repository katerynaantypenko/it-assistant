Software that is not in the self-service catalogue needs an approved licence before it
can be installed.

## Self-service catalogue

Open **Company Portal** on your laptop. Everything listed there is already licensed and
installs without approval: browsers, the Office suite, the usual developer tooling,
diagram and note taking apps.

## Requesting something new

1. Open a ticket of type **Software request** in the service desk.
2. Provide the exact product name, version, vendor and the business reason.
3. Your cost centre owner approves the spend.
4. IT security reviews the vendor for data processing and export restrictions.
5. Asset management orders the licence and pushes the package to your device.

Expect five working days end to end. Licences renew annually; you get a reminder and the
licence is reclaimed if you do not confirm that you still use the product.

## Please do not

Install unlicensed or personal-licence software on a managed device. The management agent
reports unknown executables and the ticket lands on your manager's desk.
