All floors use follow-me printing: you send a job to one queue and pick it up at any
device by tapping your badge.

## Add the queue

- **Windows** - the `\\print.example.com\FollowMe` queue is deployed automatically.
  If it is missing, run **Company Portal -> Printers -> Repair**.
- **macOS** - open Applications, run **Add Printers**, choose *FollowMe (Secure)*.

## Print and collect

1. Print to **FollowMe (Secure)**.
2. Walk to any multifunction device and tap your badge on the reader.
3. Select your job. Unclaimed jobs are deleted after 12 hours.

## Notes

- Colour printing is enabled per cost centre. If colour comes out grey, your cost centre
  has not enabled it; ask your manager to request the change.
- Scanning to e-mail sends the PDF to your own mailbox only, never to external addresses.
- Paper jams: follow the on-screen animation. If the device shows an error code, note it
  in the ticket - the vendor needs it.
