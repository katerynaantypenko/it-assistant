The hiring manager raises an onboarding ticket at least five working days before the
start date. This is what happens and what the new joiner has to do on day one.

## Prepared by IT before the start date

- Domain account, mailbox and calendar.
- Laptop, imaged and enrolled in device management.
- Membership of the team distribution list and the team channel.
- Badge request forwarded to facilities.

## Day one, done by the new joiner

1. Collect the laptop and the badge at the IT desk.
2. Sign in with the temporary password and set a permanent one - see kb-001.
3. Enroll the phone in MFA - see kb-003.
4. Connect the VPN once from the office so the device certificate is issued - see kb-002.
5. Tap the badge on any printer to register follow-me printing - see kb-005.

## Application access

Access to business applications is *not* part of onboarding. The team lead requests each
application separately, because approvals differ per system. Ask your lead which ones you
need before your first working day.
