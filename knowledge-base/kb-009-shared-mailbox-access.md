Shared mailboxes such as `invoices@example.com` are owned by a team, not by a person.
Access is granted per person and reviewed every six months.

## Requesting access

1. Open a ticket of type **Mailbox access** in the service desk.
2. Name the mailbox and the permission level you need:
   - *Read* - see the mail.
   - *Send as* - reply with the shared address as the sender.
   - *Full access* - read, send as, and manage folders.
3. The mailbox owner approves. IT does not decide who gets access.

Granted permissions appear in Outlook within an hour, sometimes after a restart.

## Using it

The mailbox shows up as an extra account in the folder list. Mail you send with
*Send as* is stored in the shared Sent Items, not in your own.

## Leaving the team

Permissions are removed automatically when you change department. If you still need
access, the new mailbox owner has to request it again.
